# 20 AI Interview Topic Ideas

Organized by category. Each is designed to be specific, conversational, and compelling.

## Tech & Software Engineering

1. **"The Moment I Realized Clean Code Was More About People Than Machines"**
   How readable code reflects empathy for future developers, and why you changed your coding philosophy.

2. **"Debugging My Worst Production Outage at 2 AM"**
   A war story about a critical failure, the systematic thinking required to fix it, and what it taught about resilience.

3. **"Why I Think Most Technical Interviews Are Broken (And What I'd Replace Them With)"**
   Your take on LeetCode culture, hiring for potential vs. performance, and designing interviews that predict job success.

4. **"The Tools I Can't Live Without and the Ones I Abandoned"**
   An evolving developer toolkit: from IDE choices to AI coding assistants, what stuck and what was overhyped.

5. **"Building for Scale When You're a Team of One"**
   Architectural decisions for solo developers, knowing when to over-engineer and when to ship.

6. **"AI as a Collaborator, Not a Replacement: How I Actually Use LLMs in My Workflow"**
   Practical daily use of AI in coding, writing, and thinking — with honest assessment of limitations.

7. **"The Open Source Project That Changed How I Think About Software"**
   A deep dive into a specific OSS project that influenced your approach to design, collaboration, or engineering.

## Business & Entrepreneurship

8. **"The Side Project That Accidentally Became a Business"**
   How an experiment turned into something real, the pivot points, and knowing when to commit.

9. **"What Nobody Tells You About Pricing Your Own Work"**
   The psychology of pricing, undercharging as self-sabotage, and learning to value expertise.

10. **"My Framework for Deciding What to Build Next"**
    How you evaluate ideas: market signals, personal interest, technical feasibility, and the "regret minimization" test.

11. **"Lessons From Failing Fast (And Why 'Failing Fast' Is Bad Advice Without Context)"**
    Nuanced take on startup mantras — the difference between productive failure and reckless failure.

12. **"Remote Work Destroyed My Routine and Then I Rebuilt Something Better"**
    The real challenges of distributed work, creating structure without rigidity, and designing a life around async.

13. **"Why the Best Businesses Solve Problems the Founder Personally Has"**
    Dogfooding as strategy, the advantage of being your own first customer, and avoiding solution-in-search-of-a-problem traps.

14. **"The Negotiation That Taught Me Everything I Know About Communication"**
    A specific business negotiation that revealed principles applicable far beyond deal-making.

## Philosophy & Life

15. **"The Book That Rewired My Brain (And Why I Re-Read It Every Year)"**
    A specific book that fundamentally changed your worldview, with concrete examples of altered decisions.

16. **"On Being a Generalist in a World That Rewards Specialists"**
    The tension between depth and breadth, navigating the "jack of all trades" label, and why polymaths may be the future.

17. **"What Running Taught Me About Software, and Vice Versa"**
    Cross-domain thinking: how training principles map onto engineering discipline.

18. **"The Conversation That Changed My Mind About Something Fundamental"**
    Intellectual humility in practice: a specific instance of updating a deeply held belief.

19. **"Why I Started Having AI Interview Me (And What It Reveals About Self-Reflection)"**
    Meta-interview about the format itself — using AI as a mirror, the Socratic method with a machine.

20. **"What I Want to Be True About My Work in Ten Years"**
    Forward-looking reflection on legacy, impact, the kind of problems worth spending a career on.
