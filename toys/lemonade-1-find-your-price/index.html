Lemonade 1: Find your price

Pick a lemonade price each day for 30 days. A cup costs $1.20 to make, and a bot plays the same days beside you with the same visitors.

Pick a price, then sell one day or several. A cup costs $1.20. A bot plays the same 30 days.

The bot uses Thompson sampling (William R. Thompson, 1933). Each day it guesses how likely a visitor is to buy at each price, picks the price that looks best under that guess, and updates.
