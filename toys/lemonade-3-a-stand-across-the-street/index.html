Lemonade 3: A stand across the street

A second stand opens across the street on day 1, and it sets its price by a rule you can see. Play 90 days by hand or write a rule, and watch how its rule moves your best price.

Pick a price each day, or write a rule. The stand across the street follows the rule shown.

The bot uses Thompson sampling (William R. Thompson, 1933). Each day it guesses how likely a visitor is to buy at each price, picks the price that looks best under that guess, and updates. The stands across the street don’t learn. They follow their rules.
