Title: The Repricer Spiral
Excerpt: Three repricing bots, each with a sensible rule, and what they do to a price in a week.
Toy type: Toy
Tags: toy

---

Most sellers on a big marketplace don't set their prices by hand. A bot does it. The bot follows a rule, and the rule usually sounds reasonable. Be a little cheaper than the next seller. Match the lowest price. Stay a bit above the cheapest, because you ship faster.

This toy asks what happens when two or three of those rules meet. Set a rule for yourself, give the two bots theirs, and press play. One tick is one minute. A week goes by in a few seconds.

**Race to the floor.** Everyone undercuts the lowest rival by a small amount. Nobody ever decides to sell at their floor price. They get there anyway, one small undercut at a time, and then they stay there. The seller with the lowest floor ends up just under everyone else.

**Sawtooth.** One bot gives up when it hits its floor and jumps back to its ceiling. The other bot then has room to climb, so it does, and the race starts over. The chart turns into teeth. Prices go up and down all week, and no person touched them.

**Matchers.** Everyone matches the lowest price. If they all start at the same price, nothing ever moves. It is the dullest chart in the toy and maybe the most useful one.

**The $23.7 million book.** In 2011 Michael Eisen noticed a developmental biology book, *The Making of a Fly*, listed for $23,698,655.93. Two sellers had it. Once a day, one set its price to 0.9983 times the other's. The other set its price to 1.270589 times the first. Each rule makes sense alone. The first seller wants to be just cheapest. The second, Eisen guessed, didn't hold a copy and planned to buy the other seller's if anyone ordered, so it priced above and leaned on its better ratings. Together they multiply the price by about 1.268 every day, and neither had a ceiling. Starting from about $35, that is $185 after a week and about $44,000 after a month. The preset runs eight weeks so you can watch it pass $20 million.

The Buy Box in this toy is a stand-in. The real formula isn't public. Here, each minute goes to the best mix of price, fulfillment and rating, and the weights are made up. Move the sliders and see how much they matter.

None of this needs a bad actor. The rules do it on their own.
