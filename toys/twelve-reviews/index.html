How you shopped

    How it works: each aisle has a typical spread of star ratings, worth about 30 reviews. A product's own reviews are added to that, so a few reviews barely move it and thousands swamp it. The ranges come from 4,000 random draws. The products and their reviews are made up from a hidden true rating, so the reveal is honest.

    The aisle priors are illustrative, not measured. Fitting them from the public Amazon Reviews 2023 dataset (McAuley Lab) is the follow-up.
