Lemonade 4: Two competitors

Two competitors sell lemonade nearby, each with a price rule that watches you and the other competitor. Some pairs of rules settle down. Others spiral.

Pick a price each day, or write a rule. Two competitors react to you and to each other.

The bot uses Thompson sampling (William R. Thompson, 1933). Each day it guesses how likely a visitor is to buy at each price, picks the price that looks best under that guess, and updates. The competitors don’t learn. They follow their rules.
